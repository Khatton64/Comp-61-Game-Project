# Zombyte

# Game Overview

**Game Title:** Zombyte 
**Genre:** Action Roguelike, Survival, Bullet Hell

**Game Summary: **
"Zombyte" is a 2D top-down survival game where players battle through endless waves of monsters in a cursed world. Inspired by *Vampire Survivors*, the player collects weapons, upgrades their abilities, and fights to survive as long as possible. Each run is randomized with different item drops, enemy spawn patterns, and environmental hazards.

**Core Gameplay Loop:**  
Players spawn into an arena and must continuously move, shoot, and dodge while defeating waves of enemies. Collecting XP gems from defeated foes levels up the player, allowing them to choose upgrades and evolve their weapon set. Survival time and kill count contribute to unlockables and new characters.

## 🕹️ Gameplay Mechanics

**Controls:**

| Action        | Keyboard     | Gamepad         |
|---------------|--------------|------------------|
| Move          | W A S D      | Left Stick       |
| Shoot         | Mouse (aim & click) | Right Stick or RT |
| Pause Menu    | ESC          | Start Button     |
| Interact      | E            | X (Xbox) / □ (PS)|

**Core Mechanics:**
- **Shooting:** Directional aiming with cooldown system.
- **Leveling Up:** Collect XP gems to gain levels and choose from random upgrade options.
- **Health & Damage:** Health bar, healing items, invulnerability frames.
- **Abilities:** Passive and active skills like dash, multi-shot, flame aura.
- **Enemy Waves:** Increasingly difficult waves with varying enemy types.

**Level Progression:**
- The game is run-based and arena-style.
- Each run increases in difficulty over time (waves).
- Players unlock new characters, weapons, and upgrades after each session.

**Win/Loss Conditions:**
- **Win:** Survive the full wave timer (if implemented).
- **Loss:** Player HP reaches zero.
- Optional: Leaderboard high scores or time trials for replay value.

## 📖 Story and Narrative

**Story Summary:**

Centuries after a catastrophic war between vampire clans and humanity, a forbidden artifact has unleashed ancient horrors upon the land once more. You are a cursed hunter, doomed to relive the same night in a collapsing realm of shadows. As you fight wave after wave, fragmented memories resurface — a dark truth hidden in blood and time.

- **Beginning:** The hunter awakens in a decrepit graveyard, surrounded by undead.
- **Middle:** Cryptic visions lead the player through lost ruins, uncovering the vampire bloodline’s secrets.
- **End:** A climactic final wave pits the player against a forgotten vampire lord. Can the cycle be broken?
